# Flux Purr v0.27.8

## Highlights

- Enforces an uninterrupted Hold lifecycle during thermal qualification and rejects observed Hold regressions instead of allowing a later Hold segment to qualify the run.
- Keeps Hold recovery inside the Hold controller while preserving temperature protection, bounded PPS pauses, and independent 60-second confirmation.
- Records fresh A-group eight-target HIL evidence covering 60°C through 220°C with continuous Hold qualification and EEPROM persistence verification.

## Release scope

This release publishes the thermal Hold qualification and runtime safety fixes merged by pull request #125, including the firmware stack-safety repair and the associated specification, implementation, and HIL evidence updates.
